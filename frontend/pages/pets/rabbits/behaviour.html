Getting to know your rabbit

Understanding Rabbit Behaviour

Rabbits communicate through their movements, posture and everyday actions. Learning to recognise these behaviours can help you understand how your rabbit feels and what they might be trying to tell you.

Binkying

A joyful jump with a twist in mid-air. Binkying is one of the clearest signs that a rabbit is excited and happy.

Flopping

When a rabbit suddenly rolls onto its side and stretches out, it often means they feel comfortable and relaxed.

Thumping

A strong thump from the hind legs can signal fear, alert other rabbits to danger or express frustration.

Nudging & Licking

Gentle nudges may be a request for attention, while licking is commonly associated with affection and social bonding.

Remember: Every rabbit has a unique personality. Always observe their overall body language and usual habits rather than interpreting one action alone.
The language of rabbits

What Is Your Rabbit Trying to Tell You?

Rabbits may not speak like humans, but their body language says a lot. From ear movements to subtle sounds, every signal can offer clues about how they are feeling.

01

Ear Positions

A rabbit's ears help them detect sounds and express their attention or mood.

  • Ears forward: Curious or listening.
  • Ears relaxed: Often comfortable.
  • Ears tightly flattened: May indicate fear or discomfort.
02

Eyes & Expressions

Their eyes and facial expressions can provide clues about their surroundings and comfort.

  • Partially closed eyes: Often relaxed.
  • Wide eyes: May indicate alertness or fear.
  • Head lowered: May be inviting grooming or petting.
03

Body Posture

The way a rabbit sits, stretches or moves can reveal how comfortable they feel.

  • Relaxed stretching: Feeling comfortable.
  • Crouched and tense: May be frightened.
  • Hiding: Could indicate stress or a need for security.
04

Sounds & Vocalisation

Rabbits are generally quiet animals, but some sounds can communicate strong feelings.

  • Gentle tooth clicking: Often contentment.
  • Grunting or growling: May signal displeasure.
  • Loud tooth grinding: Can indicate pain or distress.
Read the whole picture: One signal does not always tell the full story. Observe your rabbit's ears, eyes, posture and surroundings together to better understand how they feel.
