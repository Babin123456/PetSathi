Our Privacy Commitment in Brief

At PetSathi, we believe in honest and transparent stewardship of community data. In current development stages, PetSathi operates primarily as a static front-end educational and walk coordination preview.

We do not sell personal information, we do not monetize your browsing behaviour with invasive tracking networks, and any interactive features currently save information locally inside your own browser (via localStorage) unless explicitly sent to us via direct contact.

1

Introduction

Welcome to PetSathi (“we”, “our”, or “us”). PetSathi is an open-source, community-driven platform created to empower pet parents, dog walkers, and animal lovers across India and beyond with trusted pet care guides, walker discovery, and walk safety handovers.

This Privacy Policy sets out how PetSathi collects, uses, handles, and protects information when you browse our website (hosted at petsathi-nsoc.netlify.app) or contribute to our open-source codebase on GitHub.

Development & Prototype Stage Notice

PetSathi is actively developed as an open-source community platform under the National Social Summit / NSoC initiatives. Current frontend forms (such as login, registration, and walk handovers) operate as client-side previews or utilize browser local storage.

2

Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to fulfill your requests, assist your inquiries, and improve your navigation experience. Depending on how you interact with PetSathi, we collect the following categories of data:

Category Data Elements Actually Handled Source / Method
Contact Inquiries Full name, email address, topic/subject, and your inquiry message. Submitted by you voluntarily through our Contact Us page form.
Community Reviews Reviewer display name, star rating, service category, and feedback text. Submitted on the Reviews page. Stored in client session / preview memory.
Walk Handover Checklist Pet name, breed, special care instructions, emergency contact name and phone number. Submitted on the Walk Handover Checklist tool. Persisted strictly in your device's browser localStorage.
Notification Preferences Read/unread notification statuses and dismiss preferences. Stored locally on your device in localStorage (key: petsathi_notifications_data).
Technical Server Logs IP address, browser type, referral URLs, operating system, and timestamp. Automatically logged by standard web hosting services (Netlify CDN) for performance, uptime monitoring, and DDoS mitigation.

What We Do NOT Collect: We do NOT collect credit card numbers, payment banking details, government identification numbers, biometric markers, or precision background geolocation tracks.

3

How We Use Information

We use the limited information collected strictly for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Answering Inquiries: Processing and responding to support questions, feature suggestions, or feedback submitted through our contact form.
  • Providing Pet Care Guidance: Delivering interactive care advice, nutrition calculators, breed indexes, and behavioural tips.
  • Facilitating Walk Handover Safety: Enabling pet owners and caregivers to draft checklist summaries and emergency notes that stay on the user's browser device.
  • Platform Improvement: Debugging broken links, optimising site performance across desktop and mobile screens, and enhancing user accessibility.
  • Open Source Community Building: Welcoming volunteer contributors, documenting issues, and managing bug reports through our public GitHub repository.
4

Information Sharing

PetSathi does not sell, rent, lease, or trade personal data to third-party brokers, advertisers, or marketing agencies.

We may share or disclose information only in the following bounded circumstances:

  1. Walk Handover Coordination: When an owner fills in walking notes and emergency instructions, those details are rendered to help walkers care for the pet. In current iterations, this is done directly on the client's screen or device.
  2. Hosting & Technical Infrastructure: Trusted infrastructure providers (such as Netlify for hosting and GitHub for version control) process web requests according to their strict privacy standards.
  3. Legal Requirements: If required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority to investigate potential fraud, security violations, or imminent safety threats.
5

Data Security

We implement sensible, industry-standard technical measures commensurate with our platform architecture:

  • HTTPS Encryption: All traffic between your browser and PetSathi is encrypted in transit using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Local Storage Isolation: User preference flags and handover entries are sandboxed within your browser's origin-isolated localStorage domain, inaccessible to other domains.
  • Static Architecture: Because PetSathi's current release is hosted as a static web application without central SQL storage of user passwords, database leakage risks are substantially minimised.
  • Sanitised Inputs: Front-end form fields employ client-side validation and HTML entity escaping to safeguard against Cross-Site Scripting (XSS).

Realistic Security Disclaimer

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely infallible. While we exercise care, we cannot guarantee absolute security against all unforeseen vulnerabilities. We urge users not to submit sensitive financial credentials or unneeded personal identifiers into demo form fields.

6

Cookies & Tracking

PetSathi adopts a minimal tracking posture:

  • No Third-Party Advertising Cookies: We do not deploy advertising tracking cookies (such as Meta Pixel, Google Remarketing, or ad retargeting scripts).
  • Browser Web Storage: Instead of tracking cookies, we make use of modern browser Web Storage APIs (localStorage) to retain harmless UI preferences such as dismissed notifications and saved walk safety checklist notes across page reloads.
  • Managing Your Storage: You can inspect, view, and clear all PetSathi local storage at any time via your browser's Developer Tools (under Application > Local Storage) or by clearing your browser cache and cookies.
7

User Rights

Regardless of your geographic location, we respect your rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can inquire what personal data, if any, we hold regarding your communication inquiries.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request that incorrect contact details or feedback submitted by you be amended or updated.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your contact records or submitted testimonial messages. Furthermore, you can delete your browser-stored handover and notification records instantly by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our team.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the details below. We will review and respond to legitimate requests promptly.

8

Third-Party Services

PetSathi integrates or references select third-party resources necessary to deliver a modern, performant web interface:

  • Google Fonts: We load typography (DM Sans and Manrope) via Google Fonts servers. Google's privacy terms apply to font asset delivery.
  • Font Awesome / Cloudflare CDN: UI iconography is fetched from Cloudflare's content delivery network (CDNJS).
  • Netlify Hosting: Our static website deployment is served via Netlify, which captures standard HTTP routing logs.
  • GitHub: Community code contributions, bug trackers, and repository discussions are hosted on GitHub under Microsoft's privacy policies.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of external services when leaving PetSathi via external links.

9

Policy Updates

As PetSathi evolves—including the introduction of production backend databases, user authentication services, and walker payment flows—we may update this Privacy Policy to accurately mirror those advancements.

When changes occur, the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page will be revised. For significant architectural changes, notices will be posted within our release notes on GitHub and highlighted on the platform's notifications feed. Continued use of PetSathi following posted updates signifies your acknowledgment of the revised terms.
